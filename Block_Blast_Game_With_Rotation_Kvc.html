How to Play

1

Drag and drop blocks from the tray onto the 8x8 grid.

2

Rotation: Press R key while dragging or tap a block in the tray!

3

Clear full rows and columns to earn points.

4

When no valid moves remain, press the restart button at the top right to start over.
